## Explainable ML subtype classification + transformer-based PCM virtual screening pipeline
## Overview

This repository contains the complete, sequentially reproducible analysis
pipeline for the study linking breast cancer intrinsic molecular subtype
classification to multi-target natural product drug discovery. It covers
every computational stage from raw transcriptomic cohort preprocessing
through to virtual screening and lead prioritization.

## Objective

Breast cancer comprises five intrinsic molecular subtypes with distinct
transcriptomic programs, prognoses, and therapeutic vulnerabilities, yet
accurate subtype discrimination remains limited by boundary overlap and
poor cross-cohort generalizability, while single-target therapeutic
strategies often fail against pathway redundancy and acquired resistance
in the highest-need subtypes (Basal-like and HER2-enriched).To address this,
We developed an integrated computational framework linking transcriptomic-classification,
explainable target prioritisation, and multi-target natural-product discovery.
